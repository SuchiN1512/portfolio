How to read these

Each case study is built around what hiring managers actually look for.

Research judgment
Every study leads with how I scoped the question, the method I chose, and the alternatives I ruled out — and why.
Impact over activity
Each case ends on the decision it changed: a shipped feature, a re-sequenced roadmap, a launch de-risked.
Method nuance
Mixed, generative, evaluative, and scrappy work — with the reasoning behind each method sequence, not a default playbook.
Business fluency
Research framed around what was at stake for the product and the business, not activity counts.
AI with judgment
Where I used AI to accelerate analysis, and where I kept the interpretation human.
Reflection
Every study closes with what I'd do differently — the part that shows how I learn.
