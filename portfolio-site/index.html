The short version

A resume tells you what I did. This tells you how I think.

I spend my time getting close to people — through cross-market interviews, diary studies, and field work — then translate the messy, human truth into research that shapes how AI products get built. Before research, I was a designer and data visualizer, so I don't just hand over findings. I make them impossible to ignore.
